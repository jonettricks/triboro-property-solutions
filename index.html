Three Paths. One Goal.

Find the structure that works for you.

Price matters. But so do timing, repairs, monthly carrying costs, taxes, an existing mortgage, tenants, and what you need to do next. We look at the entire situation before recommending a path.

01

Cash / As-Is Purchase

For sellers who place the highest value on speed, simplicity, and avoiding repairs or extensive preparation.

Best when certainty matters most
How this works
A cash or as-is purchase can be the simplest route when you want to avoid repairs, showings, financing delays, or a long closing process. The tradeoff is that speed and convenience can affect price. We explain the numbers clearly so you can decide whether the certainty is worth it for your situation.
02

Lease Purchase

For situations where preserving more of your desired price may matter more than receiving all of the proceeds immediately.

Best when price matters most
How this works
A lease purchase can give a seller more flexibility when getting closer to the desired price matters more than receiving every dollar immediately. The purchase price, timing, and responsibilities are agreed to in writing up front. You still get to review the structure and decide whether it is comfortable for you before moving forward.
03

Owner Financing

For properties and sellers where a terms-based transaction may create flexibility that a conventional purchase cannot.

Best when terms create opportunity
How this works
With owner financing, the seller may agree to receive some of the purchase price over time instead of requiring a bank to provide all of the financing at closing. That can create flexibility around price, timing, and monthly payments. Any terms are spelled out in writing so you can evaluate exactly what you are agreeing to.
Not sure which option fits? You do not need to be. That is our job. We first learn about the property and what you are trying to accomplish, then explain any options that may make sense. You decide whether you want to take the conversation any further.

Straightforward By Design

Creative does not mean complicated.

Sometimes a traditional sale is the right answer.

If listing the property and waiting for a conventional buyer clearly gives you the best outcome, that may be exactly what you should do. Our role is to see whether another path creates an advantage worth considering.

A creative purchase changes the structure, not the need for clarity.

The important questions remain simple: What are you receiving? When are you receiving it? What responsibilities remain? And what protections are contained in the written agreement?
